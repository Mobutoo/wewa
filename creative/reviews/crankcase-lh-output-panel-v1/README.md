P18 — LH rear/output exterior casting panel, original bounded study

Private physical reference PF541109-7637844602 shows the rear/output exterior region next to the open magneto cavity. This new component follows that projected region only: shaped perimeter, raised circular seat, six radial webs, six mounting bosses and seven through bores. It does not reproduce the retained output cover, rotor cavity, bearings, seals, serial marks, other case half, head or cylinder.

Projection scale0.45mm/pixel [0.35,0.55] is a working estimate, not photographic metrology. The rear closure thickness9mm [5,16], seat height21mm, seat bore radius19.5mm, relief amplitudes and all boss depths are estimates. The uncalibrated view does not resolve the installed section or interfaces. The left perimeter is a finite cut beside the magneto cavity; the plain back is a review closure. This is a substantial exterior fragment, not the whole crankcase or engine.

The first gray core was too flat and is retained in rejected-planar-core. The revision replaces that slab with a continuously sampled convex shoulder and lower transverse ridge, clipped to the traced silhouette. Integrated seat, tapered domed ribs and bosses are joined by exact Boolean operations before their bores are cut. The smooth core is triangulated in source and checked again in actual GLB buffers. Estimated relief is explicit, with no source photograph used as texture.

Existing historical output-cover source/review directories are absent from this checkout, although its workflow record remains. They must be supplied for later registration; the part is not recreated here. Actual case depth, opposite surface, mounting hardware, bore fits and cylinder/cover continuity remain unqualified. P18 is incomplete and has no artistic reception.

Reproduce with the existing official Blender4.3.2 runtime, --python-exit-code1, tools/powertrain_completion_p18_output_panel_v1.py and the existing staged renderer. Exact frozen protocols, source/export equality, native900×700 at96 samples, Float32 EXR headers and Babylon captures accompany the delivered candidate when complete.
