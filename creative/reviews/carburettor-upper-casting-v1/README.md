P19 — Upper region of main carburettor casting, original bounded exterior study

The privately held JV581204 photograph shows a flared inlet, an intersecting slide barrel, and a small side opening on a shaped flange with a lower pierced ear. This revision builds those regions as one closed connected casting, with open bell, barrel and side-port bores. Existing cap, elbow, manifold and sleeves are not duplicated. No supplier pixels, serial marks or labels are embedded.

All metric dimensions and relative axes are estimates, constrained only by the uncalibrated photograph and previous estimated cap size. The bell selected diameter is54mm [44,65], barrel36mm [29,43], and side opening13mm [9,18]. These are not published dimensions. The lower body, bowl, small features inside the photographed inlet, levers and hardware are excluded. The photographed internal parts cannot yet be assigned reliably to fixed casting versus moving valve. Hidden bore junctions, back walls and termination sections are provisional. No installed registration or airflow performance is certified.

The first gray attempt exposed artificial terraces where the barrel intersected the inlet. It is retained in rejected-inlet-junction-steps. The corrected build starts with a solid exterior loft, unions the branch and flange, then clears the complete flared airway. This correction retains a finite lip and connected side-port walls without overlapping internal shells. Topology/normal validation does not replace visual review.

Reproduction uses the existing official Blender4.3.2 runtime with --python-exit-code1 and tools/powertrain_completion_p19_casting_v1.py; subsequent gray/material/preview/final stages use tools/powertrain_completion_render.py. Proposed clean cast-alloy PBR is uniform, with no measured grain/alloy claim. Native900×700 at96 samples and Float32 EXRs are the final evidence target. Artistic reception, actual interfaces and device qualification remain pending. P19 remains incomplete.
