# P15 local exterior context: immutable evidence

Coordinator dispatch within user-authorized autonomous motorcycle completion. Reuse only, zero new physical IDs. Source PF161002 product photo, PF161001 separate rod/clevis photo and official current-RDC00 were actually read; only public metadata/URL/hash snapshots are stored here. No new generic source study or fabrication was used to close hidden relationships.

`parent-native-read.json` actually opens all four historical natives and records real mesh/matrix/bounds/material names and source hashes. `plan.json` records bounded local poses before composition, uncertainty and explicit exclusions. `native-links-and-transforms.json` contains exact four 4x4 matrices. `native-context-read.json` reopens the render native and verifies linked mesh/material instances; nearest-vertex distances are only an approximate separation check, not exact collision/fit proof.

`compose-export.mjs` preserves historical GLB buffers/attributes/indices/materials and round-trips them; its result is `export-integrity.json`. `check-native-glb.py` actually imports the new context GLB into Blender and compares world positions, oriented triangle connectivity and corner normals against the native instances. No geometry or historical source is saved by those checks.

`world-frame-hint-check.json` independently checks Q Euler90,0,−90 and explains why the example gives an arm-origin estimate, not a certified pivot. The projected arm/pad guides come from a related uncalibrated product pose. The separate pad's retained +Z face orientation is not a mounted upward foot plane; rotation Q alone cannot establish that relation. WorldX, installed pad clocking and root/pin/link contacts remain unknown.

`build-integration-wrapper.py` makes an additive same-pose wrapper of local objects sharing exact linked source mesh/material datablocks. `integration-wrapper-equivalence.json` verifies four identical data links/matrices. This allows the stock wholebike coordinator checker to see four direct mesh objects in a named collection; the single linked exemplar at identity passes without changing the checker. Neither wrapper adds a physical part or a second visible occurrence in one composition.

Review render source and integration wrapper are alternative representations of the same local study; do not count/load both in the motorcycle. All mutable outside ledgers/catalogues and caches are excluded from the freeze. No Git/root/shared metadata/historical source or earlier freeze was modified. No fit/artistic/G8 acceptance.
