# Related DU201016 gross exterior study1

This first gray pass contains a convex exterior cap, a tangent rounded perimeter and a finite external side wall with one open rear boundary. The radius, sag and depth are authored estimates. It is not a complete lens: fastener region, opposite notch, optical bands and molded marks are still missing. The inside, section, seat and physical dimensions remain unknown. No reflector, bulb, back plate or certification mark was invented.

build_study_v1.py is standalone from its JSON parameters and the existence of the G0–G2 plan. It creates source geometry, analytic normals, arclength disk UV and an actual GLB export. check_study_v1.mjs reads real buffers and format validation; these checks do not establish photo fidelity. render_study_v1.py produced three real12 Cycles/OIDN gray/reflection previews, all opened and read. No96 or final material is claimed. Its inherited short docstring mentions a stalk; the actual source paths, geometry, report and views are lens-specific.

Frame is Xhorizontal, Zup, exterior facing-Y, isolated and unplaced. Photos remain private and were not used as textures. Exact current RDC variant, mounted fit, artistic reception and physical iPhone performance remain unconfirmed.
